# ProScan PDF features

ProScan PDF is a document scanner and PDF toolkit for iPhone and iPad. Core scanning, OCR, conversion and PDF editing are performed locally on the device. The app contains no advertising and does not add automatic watermarks.

## Scanning

- Eight scan modes: Document, Auto, Receipt, ID, Photo, Form, B&W Text and Whiteboard.
- Multi-page document capture through Apple's native document scanner.
- PDF page sizes including A4, Letter and Legal.
- Portrait and landscape output.
- Optional page numbers and timestamps.
- Three free scans every day; Pro unlocks unlimited scanning.

Detailed guide: https://proscanpdf.com/scan-to-pdf/

## OCR and conversion

- Live camera OCR for quickly capturing visible text.
- Document OCR for extracting editable text from existing scans.
- Searchable PDF creation and in-app PDF text search.
- PDF to editable Word conversion with layout-aware positioning where possible.
- Photos to PDF conversion.
- PDF pages to images export.

Detailed guides:

- https://proscanpdf.com/ocr-text-extraction/
- https://proscanpdf.com/pdf-to-word/
- https://proscanpdf.com/photo-to-pdf/
- https://proscanpdf.com/pdf-to-images/

## PDF tools

- Extract, reorder, insert and delete PDF pages.
- Merge multiple PDFs.
- Add colored text watermarks.
- Compress scanned and image-based PDFs.
- Add signatures and text form boxes.
- Lock PDFs with a password.
- Share through the standard iOS share sheet.

Detailed guides:

- https://proscanpdf.com/organize-pdf-pages/
- https://proscanpdf.com/add-watermark/
- https://proscanpdf.com/pdf-compressor/
- https://proscanpdf.com/sign-pdf/

## ID Photo Maker

- Standard sizes: 35 × 45, 40 × 50, 50 × 50, 50 × 60 and 50 × 70 millimeters, plus US 2 × 2 inches.
- Custom sizes in millimeters or inches.
- Auto Crop with manual adjustment.
- On-device checks for face count and position, eye visibility when detectable, lighting, sharpness, background consistency and output resolution.
- JPEG or PNG single-photo output.
- Printable PDF sheets for A4, US Letter and 4 × 6 inch paper.

Detailed guide: https://proscanpdf.com/id-photo-maker/

## Storage and privacy

- Files are stored in the app's local My Files library for fast access.
- When iCloud is available, the app can maintain a private backup copy in the user's iCloud account.
- ProScan PDF does not upload user documents to its own servers.
- Sharing and iCloud backup require a network connection; core document processing does not.

Privacy details: https://proscanpdf.com/docs/privacy-and-processing.md
